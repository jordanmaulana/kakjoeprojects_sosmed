# Gaji naik, kok tetap habis?

- Workflow: 1 (topic). Stand-in topic chosen by Claude to show the workflow: "gaji naik tapi tetap habis".
- Product: Ngatur Duit
- Selling level: 2. Money habits, nobody in crisis, and the app does exactly the step the post teaches (months of spending covered).
- Point of view: a raise can leave you with *less* breathing room, because what matters is months of spending your savings cover, not the salary.
- Soft-sell angle: the app counts those months from your own records (that's how its levels work: multiples of monthly spending, not rupiah; see metodologi.md).

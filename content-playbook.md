# Content playbook

How every Instagram post for the shared account gets written. Read this before writing a
post, together with the voice doc of the product the post is about:

| Product | Voice doc |
|---|---|
| Ngatur Duit | [suara.md](ngaturduit.my.id/product_knowledge/suara.md), numbers in [metodologi.md](ngaturduit.my.id/product_knowledge/metodologi.md) |
| cariinkerja.id | [brand-voice.md](cariinkerja.id/product_knowledge/brand-voice.md), rules in [marketing-playbook.md](cariinkerja.id/product_knowledge/marketing-playbook.md) |
| AI to know | [README.md §4](aiburnout.jordanmaulana.space/product_knowledge/README.md#4-voice-rules-for-any-copy-social-posts-included) |

This file decides **how much a post sells and how it is built**. The product docs decide
**how it sounds and which facts are true**. When they disagree on wording, the product doc
wins.

Jordan's brief (2026-10-23): everything is soft selling, or no selling at all when the topic
is sensitive. Style reference: [@sisasaldocom](https://www.instagram.com/sisasaldocom/).

---

## 1. The one rule

**A post has to be worth saving with the product taken out.**

Before anything else, delete every mention of the product from the draft. If what is left
is still useful, true and interesting, the post is ready to carry a soft sell. If what is
left is thin, the post is an ad. Rewrite it.

---

## 2. Selling levels

Every post gets one level, chosen before writing and recorded in its `brief.md`.

| Level | Name | What the product gets | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | No selling | Nothing. No product name, no logo on slides, no "link di bio", no product hashtag. | Sensitive topics (§3). |
| **1** | Signature | Logo or name small on the last slide, and the site in the caption's last line. Nothing in the body. | Most education posts. |
| **2** | Tool in passing | One slide (the last) and one caption paragraph saying the product does a step the post already taught. | Posts where the product fits a step naturally. |
| **3** | Feature-led | The post is about a feature, framed around the reader's problem, still passing the rule in §1. | Rarely. A launch or a feature nobody knows about. |

**Mix, per 10 posts:** at least 2 at Level 0 or 1 with no product tie-in at all, about 5 at
Level 1, 2 or 3 at Level 2, at most 1 at Level 3. When unsure between two levels, pick the
lower one.

### How a soft sell is written

- **The product comes after the value.** Last slide, last caption paragraph. Never the hook.
- **It answers a step the post already taught.** The post says "know how many months of
  spending your savings cover"; the soft sell says the app counts that for you. It never
  introduces a new claim the post didn't earn.
- **One product per post.** The account carries three products; a post never mentions two.
- **Say the limit next to the pitch**, the way the products already do ("Aplikasi belum
  memperhitungkan utang", "Ini layanan berbayar lho ga pake trial").
- **No urgency, no pressure.** No "sekarang juga", "jangan sampai ketinggalan", countdowns,
  discounts, "DM aku", or capitals.
- **CTA is an invitation.** "Kalau mau coba, ngaturduit.my.id." / "Cek cariinkerja.id ya."
  Instagram captions don't make links clickable, so add "Link di bio" only at Level 1 to 3.

## 3. Sensitivity: when to drop selling

Go to **Level 0** when the post touches any of these:

- Layoffs, PHK waves, unemployment, someone losing income.
- People who are financially struggling: debt they can't pay, pinjol pressure, judi online
  losses, rent they can't make.
- Death, illness, disasters, accidents, national mourning.
- Mental health, burnout, family conflict.
- Religion, politics, ethnicity.
- A real person's hardship (a viral story, a comment, a DM).

Why: both products already refuse to profit from distress. cariinkerja.id tells struggling
people in writing not to buy yet ("Jangan daftar kalo kamu masih financially struggling").
Ngatur Duit's rule is "Level kekayaan itu panduan, bukan vonis. Jangan pernah menghakimi."
A Level 0 post on these topics builds more trust than any pitch could.

Also:

- **Near-sensitive topics cap at Level 1.** Pinjol, paylater and debt *education* (not a
  person's crisis) can carry a signature, never a pitch, and never speak to the reader as
  someone in debt.
- **Read the room on the day.** If a tragedy or a layoff wave is in the news, Jordan should
  hold that day's Level 2 and 3 posts. Instagram's **Scheduled content** screen lets him
  move them.
- **No "we're here for you" posts that end with the product.** Either help, or stay quiet.

## 4. Post anatomy

### Format

- **Carousel, 5 to 8 slides, 1080×1350 (4:5)** is the default. It holds a hook, the
  substance and a closing slide, and people save carousels.
- **Single image, 1080×1350** for one sharp opinion or one number.
- Every slide in a carousel uses the same size (the posting checks warn otherwise).
- **Reel** when Jordan asks for one, or when the idea needs a face, a voice or a screen
  being used (a demo, a reaction, a story told out loud). See §4a.

### Slides

| Slide | Job |
|---|---|
| 1. Hook | One line the reader would ask or say themselves, or one concrete number. Readable in a second, large type. |
| 2. Why it happens | The plain explanation. |
| 3 to 5. Substance | Concrete example in rupiah or real tasks, then the step. One idea per slide, two or three short sentences at most. |
| Last. Close | The takeaway in one line. At Level 1 to 3, the product line sits here. |

Label made-up numbers: "Ilustrasi" on the slide whenever the numbers are
made up. Never invent people ("Rani, 27…"), user counts or survey results.

### Hooks that fit the voices

- A question the reader asks themselves: "Gaji naik, kok tetap habis?"
- One concrete number: "Udah apply 200 loker, yang bales? Nol."
- A plain statement of something people get wrong, said as a fact, not as a "bukan X, tapi
  Y" contrast.

### Caption

1. **First line repeats or sharpens the hook.** Instagram shows about 125 characters before
   "more".
2. Two to four short paragraphs that add to the slides, not copy them.
3. **Sources** in one line when the post states a fact: "Sumber: OJK, Kenali Rasio
   Keuangan." Dated if it's a number that changes.
4. The soft sell (Level 1 to 3 only), then "Link di bio."
5. **3 to 5 hashtags**, specific ones. None at Level 0 that point to a product.

Caption length: 600 to 1,200 characters is plenty. The limit is 2,200.

### Visuals

Use the product's own brand file: Ngatur Duit light and blue
([brand.md](ngaturduit.my.id/product_knowledge/brand.md)), cariinkerja.id dark with purple
([brand-voice.md §1](cariinkerja.id/product_knowledge/brand-voice.md#1-visual-identity)),
AI to know paper and ink ([README §7](aiburnout.jordanmaulana.space/product_knowledge/README.md#7-brand)).
Level 0 posts carry no logo.

## 4a. Reels

Same rules as every other post: the one rule in §1, a selling level from §2, the
sensitivity check in §3, the product's voice. What changes is what Claude hands over.
Claude writes the reel; Jordan records, edits and posts it.

### Where a reel lives

```
<product>/reels/<date-slug>/
├── brief.md      # same brief as any post (§8), with "Format: reel"
├── reel.md       # script, shot list, on-screen text, audio, cover
└── caption.md    # the caption, exactly as it should appear
```

Reels go in `reels/`, not `instagram/`. The posting checks and the weekly queue only handle
image posts, and they would reject a folder with no images. Once the reel is edited, Jordan
uploads it in the Instagram app (it can be scheduled there the same way). If a finished
`.mp4` should be kept, put it in the same folder.

### Types

| Type | What it is | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Talking head** | Jordan on camera, jump cuts, burned-in subtitles. | Opinions (workflow 2), cariinkerja.id, anything in Jordan's own voice. |
| **Screen demo** | A screen recording of the product with a voice-over. | Level 2 and 3: showing the step the product does. |
| **Text on b-roll** | Big on-screen text over simple footage (hands typing, a receipt, a street), no face. | Ngatur Duit, which never speaks as its maker, and quick tips. |
| **Produced** | Animated scenes from a template. cariinkerja.id already has one (`ads/remotion/` in its repo, 9:16, 30 fps, Geist). | Launches and evergreen explainers. |

### Shape

- **1080×1920 (9:16), 15 to 45 seconds.** One idea per reel.
- **0 to 2 s, the hook:** said out loud and written on screen at the same time. Same hook
  styles as §4. No logo, no intro, no "halo semuanya".
- **Middle:** one beat every 3 to 5 seconds (a new line, a cut, a number on screen).
- **Last 3 to 5 s, the close:** the takeaway, and at Level 1 to 3 the product line. At
  Level 0, end on the takeaway.
- **Subtitles always.** Many people watch muted. Keep every word inside the middle of the
  frame: Instagram's buttons cover roughly the bottom fifth and the right edge.
- **Pace:** Jordan talks at about 5 to 6 syllables a second, so a 30-second reel holds about
  130 syllables (from cariinkerja.id's voice analysis). Write to that, then cut 10%.
- **Cover:** a frame with the hook written large in the middle, because the profile grid
  crops a reel's cover to the centre.

### Audio

- **Jordan's own voice first.** It is the brand on cariinkerja.id and the most honest sound.
- Background music only quiet and under the voice. Business accounts only get Instagram's
  licensed library, so the script names a mood ("pelan, piano, tidak mengganggu suara") and
  Jordan picks the track in the app.
- **No cloned voice.** cariinkerja.id's guide track (`remotion/public/vo.wav`) must never
  ship, and the same goes for any AI voice in Jordan's name.

### `reel.md`

```markdown
# <title>

- Type: talking head | screen demo | text on b-roll | produced
- Length: ~30 s
- Audio: <voice / music mood>
- Cover text: <hook, big>

| Time | Said (voice) | On screen (text) | Shot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | "Udah apply 200 loker, yang bales? Nol." | Udah apply 200 loker. Yang bales? Nol. | Jordan, close-up, straight to camera |
| 2–8 s | … | … | … |
| last 4 s | … | <takeaway> / <product line if Level 1–3> | … |

## Notes for recording
- <anything Jordan needs: props, which screen to record, what to blur>
```

Blur or skip anything private in a screen recording: real names, balances, email addresses.
Demo screens use made-up data, labelled "Ilustrasi" on screen like slides.

## 5. Words to avoid

These are banned in at least one product's doc and read as machine-written on any of them:

- Passive openers: "Dirancang untuk…", "Dihadirkan…".
- Paired slogans: "Bukan sekadar X, tapi Y", "Lebih hemat, lebih tenang."
- Em dashes. Use a full stop or comma.
- Three abstract words in a row: "mudah, cepat, dan aman".
- Made-up people, made-up metrics, promised outcomes ("pasti dapet kerja", "bebas finansial").
- Hype: "rahasia", "100%", exclamation marks, "wajib tahu".
- Telling the reader why it matters. Say what happens.

## 6. Workflow 1: Jordan gives a topic

Jordan says something like "bikin post soal dana darurat" or "post about job hunting in
December". Claude researches it, takes a point of view and decides the soft sell.

1. **Pick the product.** The one the topic belongs to. If none fits, it's a Level 0 or
   Level 1 post with no product tie-in.
2. **Check sensitivity first** (§3) and set the level. A sensitive topic stops being a
   selling question here.
3. **Research.**
   - Primary sources first: OJK (sikapiuangmu.ojk.go.id), BPS, Bank Indonesia, Kemnaker,
     the product's own docs and public APIs. News and blogs only to find the primary source
     or to date a trend.
   - Indonesian context: rupiah, local prices and habits (es teh, ojek online, kos, arisan).
   - Write down every fact with its link and date. Say which sources couldn't be opened.
4. **Find the point of view.** One sentence a reasonable person could disagree with,
   backed by the research. A summary of the topic is not a point of view. Good: "Gaji naik
   bisa bikin napasmu lebih pendek." Not good: "Inflasi gaya hidup itu penting dipahami."
5. **Find the soft-sell angle.** Which step in the post the product does for the reader.
   If there isn't one, drop to Level 1 or 0. Don't bend the post to make room.
6. **Draft** the slides and caption (§4), or the reel (§4a), in the product's voice.
7. **Run the checks** (§8) and open a PR with the post folder, `status: draft`.

## 7. Workflow 2: Jordan gives an opinion

Jordan says something like "menurutku orang terlalu fokus nabung, padahal naikin
penghasilan lebih penting". Claude turns his opinion into a post without changing it.

1. **Restate the opinion in one line** at the top of `brief.md`, in his words. Keep his
   phrasing in the post wherever it reads well. His lines are the strongest copy there is.
2. **Don't move it.** No stronger claim than he made, no softer one, no new conclusion.
   If the opinion seems to need a caveat, put the caveat in the brief as a question for
   Jordan, not in the post.
3. **Back it with one concrete thing**: a number from a primary source, a real example, or
   something from the product's own data. Note the honest counterpoint in the brief so
   Jordan sees it.
4. **Check sensitivity** (§3) and set the level. Opinions about hard times are Level 0.
5. **Check it against the product guardrails.** If the opinion clashes with a rule (for
   example promising a result), say so in the brief and draft the closest version that
   doesn't clash.
6. **Draft, check, PR**, same as workflow 1.

Opinion posts can say "aku" when they are written in Jordan's voice (cariinkerja.id
already speaks as Jordan). Ngatur Duit material talks only to "kamu" and never as its
maker, so a Ngatur Duit opinion post states the opinion directly to "kamu".

## 8. Before the PR

Each post folder holds the usual `caption.md`, `post.json` and images (see
[tools/instagram](tools/instagram/README.md)), plus a **`brief.md`** and, for carousels, a `slides.json` ([tools/slides](tools/slides/README.md)):

```markdown
# <post title>

- Workflow: 1 (topic) | 2 (opinion)
- Format: carousel | single image | reel
- Jordan's ask: <topic or opinion, verbatim>
- Product: <product> | none
- Selling level: 0 | 1 | 2 | 3, and why
- Point of view: <one sentence>
- Soft-sell angle: <which step the product does> | none

## Sources
- <fact>: <link> (<date>). Opened: yes | no

## For Jordan
- <anything he should decide or check>
```

Then check:

- [ ] Passes §1 with the product deleted.
- [ ] Level recorded, and §3 checked.
- [ ] Every fact has a source; every made-up number says "Ilustrasi".
- [ ] Product voice doc followed; nothing from §5.
- [ ] Image posts: `npm run ig -- check <folder>` passes, and `post.json` says
  `"status": "draft"`. Jordan switches it to `ready` once he likes it.
- [ ] Reels: `reel.md` has every beat timed, subtitles match the voice line, and the
  product never appears before the close.
